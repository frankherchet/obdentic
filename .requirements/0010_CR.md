Knowledge Architecture

1. Protocol Knowledge
   Generic and vehicle-independent:
   - BLE transport
   - adapter framing
   - CAN
   - ISO-TP
   - OBD-II
   - UDS
   - KWP2000
   - ELM-style command transports where applicable

2. Vehicle Knowledge
   Vehicle/ECU-specific:
   - ECU addressing and identification
   - supported diagnostic services
   - PIDs/DIDs
   - response layouts
   - data types
   - endianess
   - scaling and offsets
   - units
   - valid ranges
   - subsystem/group metadata
   - decoder provenance and confidence

3. Diagnostic Knowledge
   Reasoning/workflow-specific:
   - which signals should be inspected for a diagnostic question
   - relationships between signals
   - useful sampling rates
   - diagnostic sequences
   - interpretation of trends and deviations
   - suitable TUI layouts

Protocol Knowledge and Vehicle Knowledge are deterministic OBDentic
data/code. Diagnostic Knowledge primarily belongs to Codex.
