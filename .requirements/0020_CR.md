# Adapter drivers are replaceable transport implementations.

## The initial drivers are:

- Carly CUA-V200 BLE
- the existing generic BLE OBD adapter as a secondary/reference driver

## The Carly CUA-V200 driver owns:

- BLE discovery
- GATT connection
- Carly-specific framing
- adapter initialization
- transport request/response handling

## It does not contain:

- EA189 signal definitions
- VW-specific diagnostic interpretation
- diagnostic reasoning
- TUI logic
